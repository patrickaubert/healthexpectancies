# A dataset with proportions on people living in institutions in France according to Drees's EHPA survey

The EHPA survey is collected every four years. Data are available for
2007, 2011, 2015, 2019, and 2023. Numbers of people are measured on the
Decembre 31st of each year.

## Usage

``` r
FRDreesEHPA
```

## Format

A data frame with 981 observations and 6 variables:

- year:

  year of observation

- sex:

  sex ('female' or 'male' or 'all)

- age:

  age

- nb:

  number of elder people living in institutions

- poptot:

  total population by age and sexe

- prevalence:

  share of elder people living in institutions
